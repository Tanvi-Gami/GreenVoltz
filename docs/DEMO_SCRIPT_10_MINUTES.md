# GreenVoltz - Simple 10-minute demo script

## Before you start

Use a browser window sized around **430 x 932 pixels** for a phone-style
recording, or record the full screen at **1920 x 1080 pixels** for a normal
project video.

This script is written to sound natural. It does not need to be read word for
word; use it as a guide while showing the application.

## 0:00-1:00 - Introduction

**Show:** The GreenVoltz login screen.

**Say:**

> Today I’m going to show GreenVoltz, a smart charging platform for electric
> vehicle drivers and charging-station operators.
>
> Finding a charger is often harder than it should be. A driver may reach a
> station and find that every charger is busy, or pay more by charging at the
> wrong time. At the same time, station operators need to manage demand,
> energy costs, and charger problems.
>
> GreenVoltz brings all of this together in one simple experience. It helps
> drivers find the right station, choose a good time to charge, reserve a
> charger, and get back on the road. It also gives operators a clear view of
> what is happening across their network.

## 1:00-1:45 - Two experiences

**Action:** Point out the **EV Driver Account** and **Station Operator** options.

**Say:**

> There are two different experiences here.
>
> The driver sees only the things they need: nearby chargers, availability,
> reservations, payment, and directions.
>
> The station operator has a separate dashboard for managing stations,
> energy, disruptions, and performance. This keeps the driver experience
> focused and easy to understand.

## 1:45-3:15 - Finding the best charger

**Action:** Open the EV Driver portal.

**Say:**

> After entering the driver portal, we can see the current vehicle and the
> amount of charge needed.
>
> GreenVoltz gives a clear recommendation instead of making the driver compare
> lots of information. It suggests a station and a charging time based on
> travel time, charger speed, price, and cleaner energy availability.
>
> The best charging window is shown in a simple way. The driver can understand
> the recommendation immediately without studying a complicated chart.

**Action:** Click **Use My Live GPS** if location permission is available.

**Say:**

> The driver can choose to use their live location. This helps GreenVoltz find
> the nearest station and update the estimated travel time.
>
> If location access is not available, the app still works using the
> Gandhinagar demo location. So the experience does not stop just because GPS
> permission was declined.

## 3:15-4:45 - Map and nearby places

**Action:** Show the map and select a station marker.

**Say:**

> The map is the main way to explore charging stations. We can see the
> Gandhinagar stations in one place and select the one we want.
>
> The selected station shows useful details such as the number of available
> chargers, charging speed, price, and how many vehicles are waiting.
>
> The waiting number helps the driver make a better decision. They can choose
> a station with less waiting instead of discovering the queue only after
> arriving.

**Action:** Click **Nearby refreshments**.

**Say:**

> Charging takes time, so GreenVoltz also shows places nearby where the driver
> can get coffee, food, or other refreshments.
>
> These places are linked to the selected station, so the driver does not have
> to search separately.

**Action:** Click **Navigate**.

**Say:**

> When the driver is ready, the Navigate button opens directions to the
> selected charging station in Google Maps.

## 4:45-6:45 - Reserving a charger

**Action:** Click **Reserve charger**.

**Say:**

> Now let’s reserve a charger.
>
> Before confirming, the driver gets a quick summary of the booking: the
> vehicle, station, charging time, distance, expected energy, waiting list,
> and estimated cost.
>
> This makes the booking clear before the driver commits to it.

**Action:** Click **Proceed to Payment**.

**Say:**

> The next step is a simple demo payment screen. This is only for the
> hackathon demonstration and does not charge real money.
>
> The QR code is connected to this reservation and shows the station, booking
> reference, and amount. It demonstrates what the payment step could look like
> in a finished product.

**Action:** Click **I've completed payment**.

**Say:**

> After the demo payment is completed, the booking is confirmed.

## 6:45-7:30 - Booking confirmation

**Show:** The confirmation screen.

**Say:**

> The confirmation screen clearly shows that the booking is confirmed.
>
> It includes the booking ID, station, time, and amount. The driver can also
> navigate to the station immediately from this screen.
>
> If something goes wrong with the booking, the app shows an error instead of
> pretending that the reservation was successful.

## 7:30-8:45 - Operator dashboard

**Action:** Return to login and choose **Station Operator**. Open the operator
dashboard.

**Say:**

> Now let’s look at the operator side.
>
> The operator dashboard gives a wider view of the charging network. It shows
> active charging sessions, available chargers, station activity, utilisation,
> and the overall health of the network.
>
> This is information the station team needs to keep the network running
> smoothly, but it is kept separate from the driver’s journey.

## 8:45-9:30 - Energy and optimisation

**Action:** Open **Energy Insights**.

**Say:**

> Energy Insights helps the operator understand demand, renewable energy,
> carbon impact, and charging costs throughout the day.
>
> This helps the network choose better times to charge and make better use of
> cleaner energy.

**Action:** Open **Optimization**.

**Say:**

> The optimisation view helps balance charging needs with charger availability,
> deadlines, cost, and sustainability.
>
> The goal is not just to charge vehicles, but to do it in a smarter and more
> efficient way.

## 9:30-10:00 - Disruption recovery and closing

**Action:** Open **Disruptions** and run the demonstration disruption/recovery
actions if available.

**Say:**

> Finally, if a charger stops working, GreenVoltz can show which charging
> sessions are affected and suggest another arrangement.
>
> So the complete journey is simple: find a charger, choose a suitable time,
> reserve it, pay through the demo flow, get directions, and keep the network
> running efficiently.
>
> GreenVoltz makes EV charging easier for drivers and easier to manage for
> station operators.

## Short backup lines

- **If GPS is denied:** “The app is using the Gandhinagar demo location, so we
  can continue without live location.”
- **If the map is unavailable:** “The station details remain available, and
  navigation can still be opened through Google Maps.”
- **If the backend is unavailable:** “The app shows the problem clearly and
  does not claim that a booking was completed.”
